Privacy policy for our mobile games

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy covers these Android games: Sand Gate, Sand Tilt, Goo Build, Topple Sort and Drop the Box.

What the games store on your device

The games keep your progress (levels, stars, settings) on your device only. You do not create an account. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google processes data such as your device advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and app usage. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first through Google's consent message. Without consent, ads are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy settings, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalized ads; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalized ads and fraud prevention. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the games, for example which levels they start, finish or fail, and how they react to ads. Firebase collects an app instance ID, device data and these game events. We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports (device state and the error), and Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings. Legal basis: consent for analytics (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); legitimate interest for crash reports and app stability (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google keeps analytics data for up to 14 months.

Processors and transfers

Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited process this data for us. Google may process data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Children

The games are for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. To ask us to delete data linked to your app instance, write to info@067.studio and name the game. You can complain to the Polish supervisory authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.

Changes

We update this page when the games change how they use data. The date at the top shows the current version.
