Spell Death word lists

The word lists that the spelling languages of Spell Death use, as shipped in the game: one word per line, upper case, folded to the game's letter cards. Each archive also holds the offensive-word filter and the compound pairs for that language. Sources and licences are in LICENSES.txt.
