Spell Death — word lists for the gameplay languages
====================================================

Each language's word store (<code>.bin) is built from the source below by
tools/gameplay_langs/ (normalize, expand, pack). The stores are modified
versions of the sources: letters are upper-cased and folded to the tile
alphabet, offensive words are removed, and words longer than the cap are cut.

English (en.bin)
  NWL2023, NASPA Word List 2023. Shipped since before gameplay languages.
  Licence status: see docs/research/gameplay-languages.md section 8.

German (de.bin, compounds_de.txt)
  enz/german-wordlist (Tanglet), https://github.com/enz/german-wordlist
  Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).

Offensive-word filters (offensive_<code>.txt)
  Based on "List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words",
  https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  Changed: upper-cased, folded to the tile alphabet, and curated (entries
  that are not offensive in game use were removed; each file lists them).
